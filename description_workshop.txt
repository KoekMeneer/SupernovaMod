[h1]Supernova Mod[/h1]

[url=https://discord.com/invite/T68d6t2bG6][b]Join our Discord[/b][/url] | [url=https://terrariamods.wiki.gg/wiki/Supernova_Mod][b]Visit our Wiki[/b][/url]

Supernova Mod is a dynamic content expansion that introduces new bosses, abilities, items, and progression layers to your Terraria experience.
It introduces the [b]Ring System[/b], which alters your combat strategies and allows for new, powerful builds.

[h2]Content Overview[/h2]

[b]New Bosses[/b]
Challenge 4 unique bosses with distinct mechanics and rewards.

[b]140+ New Items[/b]
[list]
[*] Weapons, accessories, and materials to enhance your experience
[*] Gear that ties directly into the Ring System for better build customization
[/list]

[b]16+ New NPCs[/b]

[b]Mod Compatibility[/b]
Seamlessly works with with other major mods like:
[list]
[*] Calamity
[*] Thorium
[*] BossChecklist
[*] MusicDisplay
[*] WikiThis
[/list]
making it a great addition to your modded Terraria experience.

[h2]How to Get Started[/h2]
1. [b]Install tModLoader[/b]: If not already installed, make sure you have tModLoader installed (free on Steam).
2. [b]Subscribe[/b]: Simply click the [b]"Subscribe"[/b] button to add the Supernova Mod to your game.
3. [b]Launch tModLoader[\b]: After installing, open tModLoader and go to the Mods menu, then enable the Supernova Mod.
